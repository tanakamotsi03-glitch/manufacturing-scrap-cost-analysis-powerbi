## **Project Overview** ##

This project analyzes manufacturing scrap across three production plants to identify the primary cost drivers, evaluate operational performance, and recommend process improvements that reduce manufacturing waste and improve profitability.

---

## **Business Problem** ##

Manufacturing scrap represents a significant source of operational cost within production environments. Excessive scrap can result from programming errors, supplier quality issues, raw material inconsistencies, and equipment failures.

The objective of this project was to analyze scrap performance across three manufacturing plants and identify opportunities to reduce waste and improve operational efficiency.

---

## **Objectives** ##

- Analyze scrap quantities across manufacturing plants.
- Measure total scrap cost.
- Identify quality performance indicators.
- Compare plant performance.
- Estimate potential cost savings.

## **Key Findings** ##

### 1. Production Performance ###
**Finding**

Across the three manufacturing plants:

- Total Production Quantity: 27,000 units
- Scrap Quantity: 1,000 units
- Defect Rate: 3.73%
  
**Business Insight**

Although overall production volume remains high, approximately 3.73% of manufactured units are lost through scrap. Even relatively small improvements in process quality could generate substantial financial savings.

### 2. Financial Impact ###
**Finding**

Total Scrap Cost:

### $1.89 Million ###

Average Material Scrap Cost:

### $1.88K per defective unit ###

**Business Insight**

Manufacturing scrap represents a significant financial burden, indicating that quality improvements offer immediate opportunities for cost reduction.

### 3. Potential Cost Savings ###

**Finding**

Reducing scrap costs by 45% would generate estimated savings of approximately:

**Plant	Estimated Savings**

| Plant     | Estimated Savings |
| --------- | ----------------: |
| Dubai     |         **$295K** |
| Sharjah   |         **$289K** |
| Abu Dhabi |         **$263K** |

Total Estimated Savings

**$848K**

**Business Insight**

A targeted scrap reduction strategy has the potential to recover nearly $850K in manufacturing costs without increasing production volume.

## **Recommendations** ##

### 1. Improve CNC Programming Standards ###

Programming errors were identified as one of the major contributors to manufacturing scrap.

**Recommendation**

Introduce standardized CNC programming procedures, peer reviews, and operator training to reduce programming-related defects.

### 2. Strengthen Supplier Quality Management ###

Material quality inconsistencies increase production defects.

**Recommendation**

Implement stricter supplier quality inspections, incoming material verification, and supplier performance monitoring.

### 3. Standardize Raw Material Verification ###

Mixing incompatible materials contributes to unnecessary scrap generation.

**Recommendation**

Introduce standardized material identification procedures and barcode verification before production begins.

### 4. Increase Preventive Maintenance ###

Equipment wear can reduce machining accuracy and increase defect rates.

**Recommendation**

Expand preventive maintenance schedules to minimize machine-related quality issues and reduce unexpected production defects.

### 5. Establish Scrap Reduction Targets ⭐ ###

**Recommendation**

Set a company-wide objective to reduce scrap costs by 45%, with regular KPI monitoring across all manufacturing plants.

**Business Value Recommendation**

**Business Value: By implementing the recommended process improvements and achieving a 45% reduction in scrap costs, the organization could realize approximately $848K in cost savings across the Dubai, Sharjah, and Abu Dhabi plants while improving manufacturing efficiency and product quality.**
